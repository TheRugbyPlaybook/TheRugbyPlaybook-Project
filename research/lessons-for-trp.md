# Lessons for TRP

> This file turns research into **general, original lessons** for TRP. Lessons describe principles, not copied features.

## Good lesson vs. bad lesson
- ✅ "Parents value short skill tips they can practise with their child." (general principle)
- ❌ "Use the same 'Coach's Corner' panel layout as Competitor X." (copies a distinctive feature)
