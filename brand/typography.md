## Requirements to check when choosing
- The licence allows commercial eBook use. `TO BE CONFIRMED` per font.
- The font is available in Canva.
- It is legible for early readers (e.g. clear letterforms, a single-storey "a" for 3-6 is worth considering).
- Good contrast against backgrounds (see `brand/colour-system.md`).
