## Rules
- The style must be original. Do not name or imitate competitor books or living artists in prompts.
- Show rugby technique accurately (body position, ball carrying, safe tackle technique).
- Representation must be diverse and inclusive.

## How to decide
1. Claude prepares 2–3 written style options with sample prompts. **Proposals only.**
2. The owner chooses. A small test set may be generated in OpenArt only with the owner's go-ahead.
3. The approved style is recorded here with the date.
