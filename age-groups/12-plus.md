# Age Group: 12-plus (Teen and Older Players)

> Product ID code: `12-PLUS` in product IDs · Folders: `books/12-plus/`, `characters/12-plus/`, `illustrations/12-plus/`

## Reader profile
Independent readers playing youth, school or club rugby, plus older beginners. They want credible, practical guidance and are put off by anything that feels childish.

## Content focus (starter guidance)
- Advanced skills, positional play, tactics, game understanding, fitness basics and mental skills (confidence, focus, handling pressure).
- Law and safety content must be accurate and current for the target region. Region and law source are `TO BE CONFIRMED`.
- Wellbeing: concussion awareness, recovery and respect. Health content must be general, not medical advice.

- Contact technique is accurate and emphasises safety.
- Concussion and injury messaging is responsible and points to qualified professionals.
- No unsafe training or supplement advice.
