# Age Group: 7-12 (Junior Players)

> Product ID code: `7-12` · Folders: `books/7-12/`, `characters/7-12/`, `illustrations/7-12/`

## Reader profile
Early independent to confident readers, many playing junior or mini rugby. They want stories with action plus practical skills they can try.

## Content focus (starter guidance)
- Core skills: passing, catching, evasion, support play, positional basics, and an introduction to the laws.
- Contact skills only where appropriate to the junior game. Safe technique must be shown and explained. Specific law variations for the target region are `TO BE CONFIRMED`.
- Themes: teamwork, resilience, fair play, respect for referees and opponents, practice and improvement.
