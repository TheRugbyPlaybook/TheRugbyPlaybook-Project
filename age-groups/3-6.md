# Age Group: 3-6 (Early Years)

> Product ID code: `3-6` · Folders: `books/3-6/`, `characters/3-6/`, `illustrations/3-6/`

## Reader profile
Pre-readers and very early readers. Books are usually **read aloud by a parent, carer or teacher**. Children engage through pictures, rhythm, repetition and simple actions.

## Content focus (starter guidance)
- First rugby ideas: running with the ball, passing to a friend, catching, teamwork, taking turns, being kind, having fun.
- Non-contact, play-based rugby only. No tackling technique.
- Emotional themes: trying again, sharing, being brave, being a good friend.

## Safety checklist

- No unsafe play.
- Messages are positive and inclusive.
