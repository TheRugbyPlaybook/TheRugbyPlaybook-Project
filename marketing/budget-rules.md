# Budget Rules

> **No money is spent without the owner's explicit approval.** All limits below are `TO BE CONFIRMED`.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly total marketing budget | `TO BE CONFIRMED` |
| Maximum daily spend per campaign | `TO BE CONFIRMED` |
| Maximum test budget per experiment | `TO BE CONFIRMED` |
| Pause threshold (e.g. CPA above X) | `TO BE CONFIRMED` |
| Monthly tool / credit budget (OpenArt, Canva, etc.) | `TO BE CONFIRMED` |
| Currency | `TO BE CONFIRMED` |

## Approval process
1. Claude proposes spend with its rationale.
2. The owner approves in writing (recorded here or in the campaign entry).
3. Spend is tracked in `campaign-data.md`.

## Hard rules
- Claude never launches paid campaigns, increases budgets, buys credits or subscribes to tools on its own.
- Any change to an approved budget needs fresh approval.
