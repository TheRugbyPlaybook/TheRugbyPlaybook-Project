# Campaign Rules

These guardrails apply to every campaign, organic or paid.

## Approval
- [ ] The owner approves creative, copy, audience, budget and dates **before** launch.
- [ ] No campaign is launched, edited or scaled by Claude without explicit instruction.

## Content
- Follow `brand/brand-bible.md` for voice and visuals.
- Use original content only. Do not copy competitor ads, slogans or visuals.
- No real players, teams, logos or trademarks without permission.
- Make only claims that are truthful and can be substantiated (e.g. no "best rugby book" without evidence).
- Prices and offers must match the storefront exactly.

## Audience
- Target adults (parents, coaches, educators). Do not target children directly.
- Follow each platform's policies for child-related products.

## Data and privacy
- Do not upload customer lists to ad platforms without the owner's approval and a lawful basis.

## Tracking
- Every campaign gets an ID and an entry in `campaign-data.md`. Campaign ID format: `TO BE CONFIRMED` (suggestion: `CMP-YYYYMM-<CHANNEL>-<SLUG>`).
